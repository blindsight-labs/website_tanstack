## Principles

### **Appeal** **to** (decisive&informed) **Action**
*   Tone must appeal to action without appealing to fear or inciting too much urgency
*   Urgency must be used sparingly, to have effect/be taken seriously
*   "Act while it still matters" (maybe especially for Leadership)
### **Three levels of Abstraction** (and Awareness/Expertise)
*   Ideally, we'd write each topic in three "tiers" or levels of abstraction

| Tier | Audience | Format (non.binding) | "Format" |
| ---| ---| ---| --- |
| N00b | Public, low expertise | Posts, short videos, article "intros/TLDR" | Story-based, use analogy +, has actionable takeaway |
| Intermediate | professionals, managers, enthusiasts | Blog, Linkedin, Talk-segments/bits | How it works, implications, Appeal to Action |
| technical | experts | Research Notes, White papers | Formal, Data-drive, "restrained"/curated |

The same Brand Personality can address different audiences in different ways, it's dynamic, and the sum of its communications make up for the personality. So for example:
*   N00b/the Public - Reassuring, curious, empathetic
*   Intermediate/Students&Enthusiasts/some professionals - encouraging, energetic, guidance
*   Pros&Experts - respectful, collaborative, factual

### both **Bold and Humble** (Experts with Beginners' Mindset)
the **Brand** **is** **Bold**, the founders Humble.

We can express vision-statements like "We want to set the standard for AI Integrity worldwide" as a Company because it inspires us to go beyond our individuality and aim for something bigger.

### Content System Sample
The Content System relies on dedicated writing time, in the future perhaps even a week every Quarter or similar, so that we can create Long Form Content from which we derive all the other content.
We also want to ensure that the same intellectual work (whitepaper) serves every audience.

#### Workflow and Posting Cadence
*   **Quarterly** (the Anchor for all comms)
    *   Whitepaper
    *   Video
*   **Monthly**
    *   1-2 long form posts (Blindsight's, Sky can add his)
*   **Weekly**
    *   1-2 shorter, derivative posts or snippets

Long Post → Shorter Post → One Liners/paragraphs → PR blurb
